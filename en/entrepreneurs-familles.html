Entrepreneurs

An entrepreneur's wealth does not stop at the company's door

An entrepreneur's wealth is often intertwined: the company, remuneration, treasury, real estate, taxation, family, social protection, business partners, transmission. Each element depends on the others, and decisions taken on one shift the balance of them all.

This is what makes an entrepreneur's wealth choices both decisive and difficult to isolate. Should you pay yourself more, or capitalise within the company? What should be done with surplus treasury? How do you protect yourself and your family without weakening the business? How do you prepare a sale — and above all, what comes after it?

Arius helps clarify this organisation and take coherent decisions across private and business wealth. The firm intervenes ahead of the major milestones, structures the thinking, then remains present over time to adjust the strategy as the entrepreneurial journey unfolds.

Family wealth

Preserve, organise, transmit

A family with wealth must hold three requirements together: preserving what has been built, organising what exists, and transmitting what must be passed on. Decisions must reckon with family balances, taxation, civil law, the nature of the assets, the long term — and sometimes several generations.

These subjects are rarely urgent, and that is precisely their difficulty: they must be prepared while nothing yet demands it. A successful transmission is built years in advance; family governance is designed before disagreements arise, not after.

Arius supports families in a spirit of clarity, coordination and continuity: consolidating the vision of the family's wealth, putting the right questions on the table at the right time, coordinating advisers, and giving each generation the legibility it needs to decide with confidence.
