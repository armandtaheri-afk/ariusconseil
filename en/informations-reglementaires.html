Remuneration arrangements

The remuneration arrangements, the possible existence of commissions or retrocessions, and whether the investment advice is provided on an independent or non-independent basis are set out in the regulatory documents provided to the client before any personalised recommendation is made.

Depending on the nature of the assignment, the firm may work on a fee basis, in particular for a wealth audit, a structuring assignment, family-office-style support or long-term wealth follow-up. The terms of engagement are defined according to the nature of the assignment, the client's expectations and the applicable regulatory framework.

Legal and tax advice

Legal and tax consultations are provided within the framework of the firm's principal activity of wealth advice and within the limits of the appropriate legal competence. Where the situation requires it, Arius works in coordination with the competent legal, tax or accounting advisers.
