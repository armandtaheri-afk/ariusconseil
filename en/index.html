A conviction

Important wealth decisions can never be taken in isolation

Wealth is rarely built around a single subject. It connects a family, a business, financial assets, real estate, a tax position, a civil-law organisation, transmission plans, personal risks — and sometimes several countries.

Each of these dimensions influences the others. An investment decision carries tax consequences; the sale of a business raises the question of the family's protection; a move abroad redraws the entire wealth organisation. Treating these subjects separately means taking the risk of incoherence.

Arius intervenes to provide a global reading of the situation, prioritise the issues at stake and coordinate decisions over time. The firm does not start from a product, but from a situation — yours.

The method

A family office approach applied to private, entrepreneurial and family wealth.

Inspired by family office methods, Arius acts as a point of coordination for wealth matters. The firm helps its clients consolidate their overall vision, structure their decisions, engage with their advisers and anchor their choices in a long-term strategy.

This approach means working hand in hand with the client's usual advisers — notaries, lawyers, accountants, private banks, insurers, asset managers — so that every decision fits into a legible whole: wealth governance, long-term allocation, protection of family interests, and continuity between private, business and family wealth.
