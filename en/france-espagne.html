France & Spain

Advising wealth between France and Spain

Between Strasbourg, Madrid and Nice, Arius supports entrepreneurs, families and private investors whose financial lives unfold on both sides of the Pyrenees — and internationally.

What is at stake

Two countries, one strategy

Arius supports clients whose wealth situation has a Franco-Spanish or international dimension: residence, family, real estate assets, investments, company, taxation, transmission, mobility. As soon as wealth touches two jurisdictions, every decision taken in one country produces effects in the other.

A move to Madrid changes tax residence and the treatment of income; a second home on the Costa Blanca raises succession questions specific to Spanish law; a life insurance policy taken out in France does not produce the same effects once residence has been transferred. Treated in isolation, these subjects lead to inconsistencies; treated together, they become a strategy.

The firm helps clarify the issues, coordinate local advisers and build a coherent wealth strategy, designed to withstand both time and borders.

Situations we support

Franco-Spanish trajectories

Mobile entrepreneurs

French entrepreneurs established or active in Spain: structuring of the activity, articulation of remuneration, social protection, residence choices, coherence between the two systems.

Families with assets in several countries

Families holding assets across borders — real estate, investments, shareholdings — seeking a consolidated view and a legible organisation of the whole.

International investors

Investors whose personal or professional mobility calls for wrappers and contracts suited to international situations, particularly in terms of portability and legal certainty.

Cross-border transmissions

Anticipating transmission in a Franco-Spanish context: applicable succession laws, taxation in both countries, protection of the spouse, organisation of gifts and holdings.

The role of Arius

A wealth coordinator, on both sides of the border.

Arius does not take the place of the competent legal, tax or accounting advisers in each country, but acts as a wealth coordinator to bring coherence to the decisions taken.

Where the situation requires it, the firm works in coordination with the lawyers, notaries, asesores and accountants competent in each jurisdiction, so that each adviser's recommendations fit into an overall strategy — yours.
