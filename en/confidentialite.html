Data controller

Personal data collected on this website is processed by Arius Conseil, a société par actions simplifiée whose registered office is at 4 Place de la Grande Boucherie, 67000 Strasbourg, France, acting as data controller.

Data collected and purposes

Data collected via the contact form (name, email, phone, subject, message) is used exclusively to respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, to arrange an initial conversation. It is neither sold nor used for third-party commercial purposes.

In the context of an advisory relationship, additional data may be collected under applicable regulatory obligations (client knowledge, anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing rules). These processing operations are the subject of specific information at the start of the relationship.

Legal basis and retention periods

The processing of contact enquiries is based on the firm's legitimate interest in responding to the requests addressed to it. Contact data is kept for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry, then for 3 years from the last contact. Data processed under regulatory obligations is kept in accordance with the applicable statutory periods.

Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the French Data Protection Act, you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability with regard to your personal data.

These rights may be exercised by writing to: contact@ariusconseil.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

Security and confidentiality

Arius Conseil implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. The confidentiality of wealth situations is at the heart of the firm's commitments.
